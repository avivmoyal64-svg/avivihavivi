Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account data (only if you create an account): your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash, never readable by us) and your display name.
  • Game data: your picks and the time you saved them. Your display name, picks and coins are public on the scoreboard.
  • Contact messages you send through the Contact page (your message and optional email).
  • On your device: your display name and a login session are kept in your browser storage so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not run analytics.

Why we use it

To run your account, the prediction game and the scoreboard, to reply to messages, and to keep the site secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Account and game data is stored with Supabase (database and login). The site is hosted by Vercel, which may process technical data such as IP address in server logs. Fonts or scripts may be loaded from public CDNs. These providers process data under their own privacy terms and may store it outside your country.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and game data until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself at any time from the account box on the home page (Delete account). This permanently erases your login, display name and picks. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Use account deletion or the Contact page to exercise these rights.

Children

The account features are not for anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and database access rules ensure you can only change your own picks. No system is perfectly secure, so use a unique password.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
